SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) is a private deemed university based in Kattankulathur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu. It was founded in 1985 as SRM Engineering College by T. R. Paarivendhar.

Key Details & Overview

• Establishment & Status: Founded in 1985; holds a NAAC A++ grade and Category I University status from the UGC / Ministry of Education.
• Campuses:
	• Main Campus: Kattankulathur, Chennai (250 acres)
	• Other Campuses: Ramapuram (Chennai), Vadapalani (Chennai), Tiruchirappalli (Tamil Nadu), and Delhi-NCR (Modinagar, Uttar Pradesh), along with sister/associated campuses.
• Faculties & Programs: Offers over 100 undergraduate, 140 postgraduate, and doctoral programs across six core faculties: Engineering & Technology, Management, Medicine & Health Sciences, Science & Humanities, Law, and Agricultural Sciences.
• Rankings:
	• Placed 12th among universities and 21st overall in the NIRF 2024 rankings.
	• Accredited by ABET and IET for engineering programs.
• Student Body & Research: Hosts over 50,000+ full-time students and extensive research/innovation centers, filing hundreds of patents and collaborating with major industry recruiters like Amazon, Google, and Deloitte.
